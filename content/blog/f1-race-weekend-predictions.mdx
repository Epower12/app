If you've been predicting F1 on YourFriendsLeague, you know the drill: pick your P1, P2, P3, done. It worked, but it left a lot of the race weekend on the table. Pole position, fastest lap, whether the safety car comes out — none of it counted.

Not anymore.

## Predict the whole Top 10

Instead of three dropdowns, you now build out your **entire predicted Top 10** — tap drivers in, reorder them, swap them out. Scoring is distance-based, so a near-miss still pays:

| How close your pick is | Points |
|---|---:|
| Exact position | +5 |
| Within 1 position | +3 |
| Within 2 positions | +2 |
| Correct driver, anywhere else in the Top 10 | +1 |

Predicted a driver who finishes 11th or retires? That slot just scores zero — no penalty, no drama.

## Your Top 10 pick does double duty

Here's the part we're proud of: you don't fill out ten separate questions. Your Top 10 order **automatically** covers two of the biggest bonus questions:

- **Race winner** (+3) — whoever you have in P1
- **Full podium** (+5 exact order, +3 for the right three drivers in any order)

One prediction, multiple scoring angles. No extra clicks.

## New bonus questions

On top of that, you can now call:

- **Pole position** (+3)
- **Fastest lap** (+3)
- **First retirement** (+2)
- **Safety car — yes or no** (+2)

And if you want to go deeper, there's an optional extras panel for **most positions gained**, **biggest position loss**, **winning margin**, and **retirement count** — each worth a couple more points for the sharper predictors in your league.

## League organizers are in control

Not every league wants all ten questions live. Organizers can now switch individual bonus questions on or off for their league from the **Manage** page — so a casual league can keep it simple (just the Top 10 and the core bonuses), while a hardcore league can turn everything on.

## Season finale doubles everything

We also added a multiplier system: mark a race as your league's **season finale** and every point scored that weekend counts **double**. Perfect for making the last race actually decide the championship.

## Authentic F1 styling

Small touch, but one we liked: driver picks now carry real constructor livery colors — Red Bull navy, Ferrari red, McLaren orange, and so on — so your grid actually looks like a grid.

## How to use it

1. Open any Formula 1 or MotoGP league's **Predictions** page
2. Click **Predict this weekend** on any race
3. Tap drivers into your Top 10, reorder as needed
4. Fill in pole, fastest lap, first retirement, and safety car if you're feeling confident
5. Save — you can edit right up until lights out

League organizers: head to **Manage → Driver Roster** to choose which bonus questions count for your league.

Go build your grid. Lights out is coming.
