If you've been waiting for the IIHF World Championship to make YourFriendsLeague worth setting up — wait's over.

We just shipped **Tournament Presets**. One click in the league manager, and all **56 IIHF 2026 group stage games** are added to your league with the correct teams, dates, times, and venues. Zero manual entry.

## What's in the preset

- All 56 group games across both pools (Zürich + Fribourg, Switzerland · May 15–26, 2026)
- Correct CEST kick-off times for every fixture
- Team names matched to our flag system, so you'll see proper country flags instead of generic avatars
- Auto-detected playoffs flag for knockout-stage matches we add later

Your league members get notified the moment matches drop. They start predicting. The leaderboard fills up. You become the friend who organized this. Everyone wins.

## How to use it

1. Open your league in **Manage**
2. Click the **🏆 Tournament Presets** tab
3. Pick **IIHF World Championship 2026** from the dropdown
4. Tick the matches you want (or use **Select All** if you want the whole tournament)
5. Hit **Import**

That's it. Duplicates are skipped automatically, so if you change your mind and re-import you won't double up.

## Why the playoffs aren't included yet

The knockout bracket depends on group stage standings, which won't exist until **May 26**. The moment those finalize, we'll either:

1. Ship a follow-up preset with all 8 QF / 4 SF / 2 Bronze + Gold matches, or
2. Let you add them via the manual match form (which already handles playoff tie-blocking — knockout games can't end in a draw, and the prediction UI enforces this for you)

Either way, your group stage predictions and standings stay intact.
