# CHROMATIC VELOCITY

*A design philosophy for the cinematic stage of competition.*

---

## Manifesto

Speed leaves traces. A puck's slash, a striker's cut, a sprinter's blur — the act passes faster than the eye can resolve, and what remains is light. **Chromatic Velocity** is the visual language of that residue. It treats the typographic gesture the way a long-exposure photograph treats motion: as a layered signal where the color channels have separated, where cyan trails one direction and magenta trails the other, where the white nucleus stands between them as the moment of decision frozen in glass.

The work is built around a single monumental letterform — set massive, sliced into three offset chromatic plates as though the broadcast signal itself were still resolving. This is not random glitch. It is the result of master-level chromatic calibration: cyan rendered with the discipline of a printmaker pulling a color separation, magenta laid with equal precision, white core anchoring the composition with absolute clarity. Each plate must be perfectly registered; the offset is intentional, measured, the residue of countless tests until the eye reads it not as a defect but as motion captured.

Light is sculptural. Diagonal streaks — orange flowing one axis, cyan flowing its mirror — sweep through the composition the way stadium beams cross above an arena floor. These are painted with the patient discipline of a colorist grading a feature film: gradient mapped through multiple passes, each blur radius tuned, each opacity sampled and re-sampled until the trails read as physical light rather than mere decoration. The bottom of the canvas mirrors a polished surface: the world reflects, slightly faded, the way ice rinks and lacquered courts hold the ghost of their players.

Color speaks in three precise notes. Deep cinematic black anchors. Hot cyan signals possibility, the cold side of the spectrum where data and prediction live. Warm orange-amber signals event, the heat of action, the moment something happens. Magenta whispers between them — a single chromatic accent threaded through the typography, never elsewhere, kept rare so it lands. Together they form a palette tuned with the obsessive attention of a sound engineer balancing a mix.

Composition obeys the gravity of broadcast graphics. A central mass — the letterform — pulls everything toward it. Around it, the supporting elements arrange themselves with restraint: a quiet supporting wordmark below, sized like an opening credit, letterspaced so each character occupies its own measured volume of space. Corner markers, when used, work like the registration crosses on a printing plate — proof that someone was here, measuring, aligning, refusing to ship until every distance was exact. Nothing is decoration. Every element has been placed, removed, replaced, and replaced again until the work earns its quiet.

The result should feel like a poster pulled from the back hallway of a stadium — the kind of object designed by a studio whose work is shown at brand archives, not stock galleries. It is the residue of obsession. Held to the eye it should suggest the hours behind it: the colorist's attention, the typographer's stubbornness, the kind of expertise that produces something so confident it appears effortless. That apparent ease is the most expensive thing in the room.

---

**Visual signatures:**
- Monumental central letterform — chromatic plate separation, master-calibrated registration
- Diagonal sculpted light streaks — orange one axis, cyan its mirror, blurred with discipline
- Polished mirrored floor with vertical falloff — the arena residue beneath the gesture
- Cinematic palette: deep black, hot cyan, warm orange, single magenta whisper
- Supporting typography in letterspaced restraint — opening-credit scale, never larger
- Optional registration marks at corners — proof of the maker's hand
- Composition that pulls toward a single typographic gravity well
